I BUILD SYSTEMS WHERE AI MEETS THE REAL WORLD.

I’m Sandesh B Nayak, an Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning engineer focused on building practical intelligent systems across Generative AI, machine learning, computer vision, data analytics, automation and robotics.

I work across the complete development lifecycle — from data ingestion, preprocessing and feature engineering to model development, evaluation, retrieval systems, APIs, analytics and intelligent applications.

My projects explore how AI can move beyond isolated models and become part of useful systems — research assistants, document intelligence platforms, AI agents, business intelligence tools, computer vision applications, automation workflows and hardware-integrated prototypes.

I enjoy working at the intersection of software, data and physical systems, where engineering decisions have to translate into something practical, understandable and useful.

I am particularly interested in applied GenAI, RAG architectures, multi-agent systems, computer vision, document AI, robotics and workflow automation.
